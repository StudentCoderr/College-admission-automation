About the system

A paperless admission workflow, end to end

This portal automates the entire Diploma / Polytechnic admission lifecycle — from student registration and course selection to document verification and final approval. Every step is tracked, every decision is recorded, and students can follow their application in real time.

How it works

Admission process in four simple steps

1

Register / setup password

Create your student account. If an administrator created it, complete the one-time password setup link.

2

Fill your application

Enter personal, contact and academic details, then pick a preferred Diploma or Degree course.

Upload documents

Track your status

Help

Frequently asked questions

{{ carousel_tools() }}
Anyone who meets the eligibility shown for the chosen course. Diploma / Polytechnic programmes typically require a pass in SSLC / Class 10 with Mathematics and Science — check the course page for the exact eligibility.
Register with a valid email address and choose a strong password. If an administrator creates your account, you will receive a one-time setup link to choose your own password — passwords are never shared or stored in plain text.
A marks card / transcript is mandatory before submission. We also accept a government ID proof, a passport-size photograph, a signature, and a transfer / migration certificate where applicable. Files can be PDF, JPG or PNG up to 5 MB.
Once submitted, an application cannot be edited by the student. If the admissions office finds something missing they will request a correction, which re-opens the application for you.
Log in and open your dashboard. Each application shows its current status and a full history of changes — Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Correction Requested, Approved or Rejected.
The admissions office records the reason on your application. You can contact the office or, where seats remain available, start a fresh application for another course.
